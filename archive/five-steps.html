How I work

  1. It Starts With the Feeling

    First, we tune into what the song actually feels like: the vibe, the tension, the pulse. That sets the direction for everything that follows.

  2. Lift What’s Already There

    From there, I zero in on the parts that make your track you. The grit, the warmth, and the attitude in the lyrics. Your intention leads; the production follows.

  3. Let the Vocal Lead

    Your vocal carries the story. I shape it so the emotion hits clear, intentional, and impossible to miss.

  4. Dial In the Final 3%

    The small things matter: transitions, ear candy, shifts in momentum. Those subtle details that make the listener wanna hit repeat.

  5. Build It Together

    This is a collaborative process: making decisions that feel natural, purposeful, and true to your sound.
